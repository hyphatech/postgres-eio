<Features>
  <Feature title="Secure by default" icon="shield-check" href="/postgres-eio/guide/tls/" linkText="TLS and signing in">
    TLS, SCRAM-SHA-256 with channel binding and client certificates; a
    connection that crosses a network is encrypted unless its string says
    otherwise.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Pool and failover" icon="layers" href="/postgres-eio/guide/several-hosts/" linkText="Several hosts">
    One pool serves every domain, across several hosts, with failover and load
    balancing.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="One round trip" icon="zap" href="/postgres-eio/guide/pipelining/" linkText="Pipelining and batches">
    Statement caching, pipelining and batches.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Streams" icon="waves-horizontal" href="/postgres-eio/guide/copy/" linkText="COPY">
    COPY in and out, and rows folded as they arrive, never buffered.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="LISTEN/NOTIFY" icon="rss" href="/postgres-eio/guide/listen-notify/" linkText="LISTEN/NOTIFY">
    A listener that reconnects by itself and says so.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Failures you can act on" icon="list-checks" href="/postgres-eio/guide/errors/" linkText="Errors">
    Each says whether the statement may have run, so a retry is never blind.
  </Feature>
</Features>

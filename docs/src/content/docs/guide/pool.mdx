A pool keeps a fixed number of connections and lends one to each borrower
in turn. All connections open at startup, so a bad configuration fails at
once. A returned connection is reset (open transaction, settings, temporary
tables, locks, `LISTEN`), and the reset costs the next borrower no round
trip. One pool serves borrowers on every domain, and bounds a reconnect's
name lookup by `connect_timeout` on each of them.

## Making one

<Code code={pool} lang="ocaml" />

`Pool.create` opens every connection as `connect` does, or answers the first
error after closing those already open. It takes `connect`'s options, and
these of its own:

| Option | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| `~size` | 8 | connections, all opened at startup, and at least one |
| `~wait` | 5 s | how long a borrow waits for a connection to come free |
| `~reset` | `true` | whether a returned connection loses its session state |
| `~max_lifetime` | 30 min, ±10% | a connection older is replaced |
| `~idle_check` | 60 s | a connection idle longer is pinged |

## Borrowing

<Code code={countOrders} lang="ocaml" />

`Pool.use` returns `` `Pool `Busy `` if no connection frees up within
its `wait` (5 s, or `~wait:Mtime.Span.(500 * ms)`), and `` `Pool `Closed `` at once after `Pool.close`;
`Pool.error_to_string` prints either, and a connection's errors
too, so `Result.join (Pool.use pool f)` is printed by one function. Cancelling a borrower stops it at
once, wherever it waits: its statement is cancelled at the server, so the
transaction rolls back, and its connection is closed and made again at the
next lend.

`f` runs on the calling fiber, and the connection goes back even if `f`
raises. Pipelined answers left unread are read when the borrow ends.
A wait over 100 ms and a borrow held over 250 ms log a `warn` on the
`postgres-eio.pool` log source.

## A clean connection

Every lend is clean: no open transaction, no `LISTEN`, the connection's
original timeout, and none of a previous borrower's session state. The
reset sent at give-back is `DISCARD ALL` but for prepared statements and
plans, which every borrower shares through the statement cache:
`set session authorization default`, `select pg_advisory_unlock_all()`,
`close all`, `unlisten *`, `reset all`, `discard temp` and
`discard sequences`. It is sent and not waited for; the next lend reads
its answer, usually already arrived, and makes the connection again if the
session died meanwhile.

`~reset:false` keeps session state between borrowers, except that an open
transaction is still rolled back and `LISTEN` still ended: a pooled
connection never listens. A [listener](/postgres-eio/guide/listen-notify/)
is a connection of its own.

## Housekeeping

A fiber on the pool's switch checks idle connections a few times per the
shorter of `idle_check` and `max_lifetime`. A connection past
`max_lifetime` is replaced, the new one connected before the old one is
removed. A connection idle past `idle_check` is pinged with an empty query,
while another is idle, and replaced if the ping fails. A borrowed
connection is never touched.

<Code code={poolHealth} lang="ocaml" />

`Pool.stats` answers the pool's `size`, how many of its connections are
`idle`, how many borrows are `waiting`, and how many connections
housekeeping has `replaced`, for a health check.

`Pool.close` closes idle connections at once and borrowed ones when they
come back, never under a running transaction. A borrow waiting, and every
one after, answers `` `Pool `Closed ``.
